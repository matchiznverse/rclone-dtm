Privacy policy - DTM Backup

DTM Backup is a personal tool used only by its owner. It asks Google for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files it created itself in the owner's Google Drive.

It collects no data about anyone, shares nothing with third parties, and runs no servers: the files go straight from the owner's PC to the owner's Drive. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
